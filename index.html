How to Put DayMetrics Online for Everyone (No Localhost!)

Follow these exact click-by-click steps to get a real public https://... link in 60 seconds

STEP 1: Save this website file to your computer/phone

Click the "Click Here to Download index.html" button above. It will save a single file named index.html into your computer's Downloads folder.

STEP 2 (Option A — Fastest, 60 Seconds): Use Netlify Drop
  1. Create a new empty folder on your Desktop and name it daymetrics.
  2. Move your downloaded index.html file inside that daymetrics folder.
  3. Open a new browser tab and go to app.netlify.com/drop (sign up for a free account if prompted).
  4. Drag your daymetrics folder from your Desktop and drop it right onto the dashed box on the Netlify page.
  5. Wait 5 seconds—Netlify immediately gives you a live, public https://....netlify.app website link that works on any phone, tablet, or laptop anywhere in the world!
STEP 2 (Option B — Permanent Free GitHub Pages Link)
  1. Go to github.com and sign in (free).
  2. Click the "+" icon in the top-right corner and click "New repository".
  3. Type daymetrics as the Repository name, select Public, and click the green "Create repository" button at the bottom.
  4. On the next page, click the blue link that says "uploading an existing file".
  5. Drag and drop your index.html file into the box, then click the green "Commit changes" button.
  6. Click "Settings" (top tab) → click "Pages" (left sidebar) → under Branch, change "None" to "main" and click "Save".
  7. After 60 seconds, refresh the page to see your permanent public link: https://your-username.github.io/daymetrics/!
